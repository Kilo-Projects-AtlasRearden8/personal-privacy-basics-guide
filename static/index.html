Personal Privacy Basics

Lock down your accounts, browser, and phone — opt out of AI training and data brokers — and spot scams before they catch you. Plain English, step by step, never condescending. Published by Asdale Dynamics, LLC.

What this guide covers

  • 1. Locking down your accounts — strong passwords, password manager, two-factor authentication, account recovery, breach checks.
  • 2. Locking down your browser — tracking protection, ad blocking, cookies, private browsing, sync safety.
  • 3. Locking down your phone — screen lock, find-my-phone, app permissions, updates, public Wi-Fi, SIM PIN.
  • 4. Opting out of AI training — stop ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot, and Meta from training on your chats.
  • 5. Opting out of data brokers — find your listings and request removal, step by step.
  • 6. Spotting scams — phishing, smishing, tech support scams, red flags, and what to do if you were scammed.
  • Printable privacy checklist — one page to print and check off by hand.

How to use this guide

  • Read one chapter at a time. Do the steps as you go — you do not need to finish everything today.
  • Every name, email, phone number, and address in this guide is made up. Never copy them as real, and never type your own real details into examples.
  • Open any static/*.html file directly in your web browser. No build step, no internet required after you have the files.
  • Print checklist.html from your browser for a checklist you can check off by hand.
